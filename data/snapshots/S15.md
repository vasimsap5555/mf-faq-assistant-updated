# HDFC MF - Request Statement (snapshot, captured 2026-11-12)
Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/additional-info/request-statement

HDFC Mutual Fund can email an account statement to the investor's registered email ID.
After the first investment, an allotment confirmation is sent by email and/or SMS within 5 business days to investors who have registered an email or mobile number.
A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is sent on or before the 10th of the month following any month in which the folio had transactions.
On a specific request, the fund provides an account statement within 5 business days.
The Request Statement page links to: Account statement (HDFC MF Download Statement page); Dividend statement, Exit load statement and Capital Gains Statement (HDFC MF investor portal, investor-web.hdfcfund.com); Consolidated account statement and Consolidated realized gains statement (CAMS online).
An account statement can also be requested by SMS, through the IVR on the toll-free number 1800-3010-6767 or 1800-419-7676, or at any HDFC MF Investor Service Centre.
