# HDFC MF - Download Statement (snapshot, captured 2026-11-12)
Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/consolidated-account-statement

To download an account statement on HDFC MF's Download Statement page, the investor enters a PAN or a folio number, verifies with an OTP, selects the duration (for example the current financial year) and the statement is sent by email.
An Account Statement summarises all mutual fund transactions in the folio, including purchases, redemptions, switches, dividends and units allotted.
The statement can be downloaded as soon as the investor begins investing.
There is no charge or fee for receiving or downloading the Account Statement.
Two types are available: a PAN-based account statement covering all folios linked to the PAN, and a folio-based account statement for a single folio.
