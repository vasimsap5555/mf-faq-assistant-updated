# HDFC MF - How to get a capital gain statement (snapshot, page dated 2025-08-12)
Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india

A capital gain statement reports gains or losses from redeeming mutual fund units during a period, split into short-term and long-term gains, and is used when filing an income tax return.
It can be obtained from the registrar and transfer agents (CAMS or KFintech) using the PAN and registered email ID, from the AMC's own website, or from investment platforms in their tax or reports section.
A Consolidated Account Statement in capital-gains format covers redemptions across all mutual fund schemes linked to the PAN.
Most registrars and platforms provide the capital gain statement free of cost.
