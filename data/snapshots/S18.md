# HDFC MF - SIP FAQs (snapshot, captured 2026-11-12)
Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/faqs/systematic-investment-plan

HDFC MF's SIP FAQ notes that many AMCs allow an SIP to start from Rs. 100 in most schemes, subject to the restrictions in each scheme's Scheme Information Document, and that the commonly cited minimum is Rs. 500.
The scheme-specific minimum SIP amount should be checked in the scheme's KIM, SID or Scheme Summary Document.
