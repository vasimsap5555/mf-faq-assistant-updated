# AMFI Mutual Fund Sahi Hai - Glossary (snapshot, captured 2026-11-12)
Source: https://www.mutualfundssahihai.com/en/glossary?page=1

The expense ratio, or Total Expense Ratio (TER), is the annual cost of running a scheme and has a direct bearing on its NAV: a lower expense ratio means a higher NAV, other things being equal.
SEBI caps the TER that schemes can charge; fund-of-funds have separate limits depending on the underlying schemes.
An exit load is a charge some schemes levy on redemptions made within a stipulated period, intended to discourage selling too soon.
Exit load is a percentage applied on the NAV used for the redemption; with a 1% exit load you receive 99% of the redemption value if you exit within the stipulated period.
Most schemes other than liquid funds charge an exit load for redemptions within the stipulated period.
