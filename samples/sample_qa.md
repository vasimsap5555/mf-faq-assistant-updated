## 2. ELSS lock-in?

**Type:** answer

HDFC ELSS Tax Saver has a statutory lock-in of 3 years from the date of allotment of each unit, including units allotted through SIP or IDCW reinvestment. Units cannot be redeemed, switched, transferred or pledged until the lock-in ends.

Source: [SID - HDFC ELSS Tax Saver (Nov 21 2025)](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/SID/2025-11/SID%20-%20HDFC%20ELSS%20Tax%20Saver%20dated%20November%2021%2C%202025.pdf)  
Last updated from sources: 21 Nov 2025

## 3. Minimum SIP for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

**Type:** answer

The minimum SIP instalment for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is Rs. 100 for daily, weekly and monthly SIPs, Rs. 1,500 for quarterly, Rs. 2,500 for half-yearly and Rs. 5,000 for yearly SIPs. The minimum lumpsum application is also Rs. 100.

Source: [Scheme Summary Document - HDFC Flexi Cap Fund](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/scheme_summary/2026-08/HDFCEQ_1786689286.pdf)  
Last updated from sources: 14 Aug 2026

## 4. Exit load of HDFC Mid Cap Fund?

**Type:** answer

HDFC Mid Cap Fund charges an exit load of 1.00% if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year of allotment. There is no exit load after 1 year.

Source: [KIM - HDFC Mid Cap Fund (Nov 21 2025)](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/KIM/2025-11/KIM%20-%20HDFC%20Mid%20Cap%20Fund%20dated%20November%2021%2C%202025.pdf)

## 6. How do I download my capital gains statement?
